# 📖 The Conversation Guide

*How to have the conversations that matter most. Part of the Wavelength Series.*

## 🌊 Part of the Wavelength Series

Wavelength is a growing series of communication tools and education, built on one idea: everyone could use more support communicating clearly and kindly — and that's not a flaw, it's just true. Teachers use these skills all day in the classroom. Relationship therapists spend careers refining them. Parents, coworkers, teenagers, grandparents — everyone reaches for the right words under pressure, and support for that shouldn't be reserved for people who already have a name for what they need.

The series also treats AI-literacy as a communication skill in its own right. As AI becomes part of how people write, message, and create, learning to recognize when it's present — in a text, an email, a kid's homework, a company's marketing — is becoming just as important as knowing how to say something kindly. That matters for individuals and companies alike, at every age.

**Current tools in the series:**
- 📖 **The Conversation Guide** — 70 structured guides for hard conversations, from early childhood through adulthood
- 🖐️⚙️ **Human or AI?** — a game that teaches AI-literacy by testing (and humbling) your instincts for spotting AI-written text

More tools are planned for the series over time.

## 💬 What The Conversation Guide Does

Some conversations are hard at every age, for different reasons: telling a friend they hurt you, asking for help, setting a limit, apologizing and meaning it. The Conversation Guide is a structured library of exactly those conversations — what to say, what to watch for, and how to reflect on how it went — written separately for kids, teens, and adults.

## ✨ Features

| Section | What it offers |
|---|---|
| 🧒 Ages 3–18 | 20 conversations across Friendship, Family, Conflict, and Identity/School, each broken into three age bands — 3–6, 7–12, and 13–18 — with steps, sample phrases, and a self-check written specifically for that age |
| 🧑 Adults | 50 conversations across Romantic, Friendship, Workplace, Family, and Social relationships, using the same structure |

Every one of the 70 conversations includes:
- **Step by step** — a short numbered walkthrough
- **Say this / Instead of this** — paired real phrases, not vague advice
- **What to watch for** — signs the conversation needs a different approach
- **Reflection prompts** — questions to sit with afterward
- **Self-check** — a tappable checklist that scores how the conversation went

## 🎨 Design

A warm, parchment-toned palette instead of a clinical one — sand and cream backgrounds, with coral accents for the kids' mode and green for the adults' mode. Headlines in Playfair Display, body copy in Source Sans 3, and Source Serif 4 for the intros and scripted lines, giving the harder moments a little more weight.

## 🛠️ How It Works

Single HTML file, sidebar navigation with search, no build step and no external dependencies beyond Google Fonts. Unlike Wavelength, nothing here calls an AI model — every conversation guide is pre-written, so the whole thing runs anywhere, including hosted directly on GitHub Pages, with nothing extra required.

## 🎯 Who It's For

Parents navigating a hard moment with their kid. Teachers looking for language for a classroom conflict. Couples and friends trying to say the thing they've been avoiding. Teenagers figuring out how to ask for help. Therapists looking for a script to hand a client. Communication support shouldn't be reserved for people who already know what to call what they need — this is for everyone, at every age, having any of the conversations that are hard to start.
